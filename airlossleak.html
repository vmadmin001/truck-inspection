Air Loss/Leak- Testing Procedure

  • 1. Enter cab
    "The wheels are chocked, both parking brakes are applied, My truck & trailer secure"

  • 2. Start the engine, release both parking brakes ( Push in yellow & Red)
  • - Lower driver window, Turn Truck off & turn Ignition ON
  • - Press & hold Pedal/Service brake & state current air pressure
    "My current pressure is ___psi"

  • 3. Start Timer & Listen for possible air leaks for 1 Minute
    "For 1 min, I will listen for possible air leaks, outside"

  • 4. After 10-15sec,
    " I hear no air leaks outside, I will continue to listen"

  • 5. After 1 Minute, Stop Timer
    " My current air pressure is ____psi, I lost 2psi in 1 minute Which is better than 4psi"

  • 6. Apply both Parking brakes ( Pull out Red & yellow), Secure Truck & Trailer
  • 7. Read Minor & Major Defects
